Analysis of the impact of natural light on fabric-based artworks exhibited in the Podium hall during Fondazione Prada's Paravent exhibition.

Key aspects included evaluating the risk of photodegradation to delicate textiles due to UV exposure, as well as how natural light revealed the texture, layering, and transparency of the paravents.

Lighting simulations and renderings were used to identify high-exposure areas and propose strategies such as UV filtering or layout adjustments, balancing preservation with curatorial goals.

The analysis considered both the best and worst-case scenarios for natural light-December 21 with cloudy skies and June 21 with clear skies-along with additional sample days covering various weather conditions and exposure times, in order to calculate an average light exposure for the hall.
