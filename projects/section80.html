Section80.Bar is a hybrid space that combines culture, inspiration, and relaxation, catering to those seeking intellectual stimuli and social interaction.

Created by the Section80 team in collaboration with Stefano Pezzini (Lighting Design), Studio Wok (Interior design) and H.A.N.D. HiFi (Sound design).

The design of the building housing the bar —a piano-tail-shaped structure in the Isola district—served as the inspiration for its concept.

The idea to recreate an urban portico that continuously transforms, from the light design to the seating arrangements, reflecting the imagination and creativity of the quests.

Concept by Section80 team in collaboration with Kitchen Cabinet, La Peg, Stéphane Wargnier, and Studio Weigt.

Lighting layers create depth and visual interest.

The exterior design establishes the bar's identity on the street.

A hybrid space for art, a nightclub, a bar – a platform that echoed its values of the "indie spirit" the founders wanted to embed.

The space is secretive, private and special, almost club-like in its intimacy.

Custom lighting fixtures enhance the atmosphere.

Architectural integration of light and space.
