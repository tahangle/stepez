Lighting design for the Chaumet showroom within Selfridges, with a focus on accent lighting and the use of varying correlated colour temperatures (CCTs)

The lighting scheme integrated both ceiling-mounted and custom-built furniture lighting, carefully tailored to highlight the elegance of the jewellery pieces.

Particular attention was given to the selection of beam angles nd colour temperatures to create contrast, visual depth, and a luxurious atmosphere, while ensuring optimal product visibility and customer experience.
