Relamping and conversion for energy efficiency and minimal maintenance, including Casambi programming, following the original lighting concept of "positive contrast".

In 2006, the firm Richard Meier + Partners designed the glass structure surrounding the altar, which is housed within the Museo dell'Ara Pacis.

The new sustainable system, with 218 LED ERCO fixtures reliable and maintenance-free, boasts a lighting power of just 8.4 KW, while the previous system reached 57 KW, resulting in an impressive 85% energy saving.

Created Casambi light scenes based on the original"positive contrast" concept. Brightness levels were used to establish perceptual hierarchies,while varying color temperatures enhanced contrast. The area around the Altar is lit with warm white general lighting, while the monument is highlighted with neutral white accent lighting.
