Lighting design project for the Goyard boutique on Madison Avenue, New York City, developed for the architecture firm Moinard Bétaille.

The proposal included an innovative facade lighting concept, enhancing the store's exterior presence. Inside, the design featured recessed lighting fixtures and electrified tracks for the ceiling, light panels for wall decorations, and mini projectors with recessed spotlights to provide integrated lighting for the display areas.

The goal was to preserve the building's original architectural integrity while creating a sophisticated and functional lighting scheme.
