Photorealistic renderings of a studio apartment, created using 3DSMax software with the Mental Ray plugin, for the study of natural light exposure.

The project involved developing a 3D model of a studio apartment using 3as Max software. A real-world material was acquired and calibrated -using bump maps and bitmaps-, and lighting fixtures were integrated using photometric data based on actual lighting products.

I then produced four high-quality renderings: two with artificial lighting, one with natural lighting, and one false-color rendering. The false-color render was used to illustrate luminance levels and natural light exposure under both optimal and poor lighting conditions.
