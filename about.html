About

Lighting Designer

Born and raised in Milan, the international capital of design, Stefano Pezzini is a Lighting Designer with a background in Interior Design and a Master's degree in Lighting Design & LED Technology from Politecnico di Milano.

His passion for design began with a short course at Central Saint Martins in London, followed by a Bachelor's degree at Politecnico and an Erasmus exchange in Porto, which enriched his international perspective. A key moment came during a project in South Africa with Liter of Light, where he co-led the installation of solar lighting systems—an experience that sparked his interest in lighting design.

Since then, Stefano has worked on projects across Italy and France, blending creativity, technical skill, and environmental awareness. He continues to explore the emotional and spatial power of light through sustainable and impactful design.
